Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

eco-drive is a personal application used only by its owner. It accesses Google Drive solely to read, write, and synchronize the owner's own files.

Data access. The app requests access to Google Drive so it can mount and synchronize the owner's Drive files. It does not access Google data belonging to anyone else.

Data storage. File data and credentials are stored only on the owner's local computer. No Google user data is stored on external servers operated by the app.

Data sharing. The app does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to third parties. It does not use Google user data for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

Retention and deletion. Local files and credentials remain on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them or revokes the app's access through their Google Account permissions page.

Contact. For privacy questions, contact eco339@gmail.com.
